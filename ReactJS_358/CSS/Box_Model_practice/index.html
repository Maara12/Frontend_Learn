Batman

Job Description

Batman is a highly skilled vigilante who protects Gotham City from criminals and supervillains. He uses his detective abilities, martial arts training, advanced technology, and strategic thinking to investigate crimes and stop threats. Working primarily at night, he patrols the city, gathers information, protects innocent people, and brings dangerous criminals to justice. Unlike many superheroes, Batman has no superpowers and relies on his intelligence, preparation, physical abilities, and strong sense of justice.
